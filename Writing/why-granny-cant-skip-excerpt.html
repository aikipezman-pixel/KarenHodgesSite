Book Excerpt

Why Granny Can’t Skip

By Karen Hodges

★

In the opening of her memoir, Karen reflects with humor and gratitude on aging, faith, and the unexpected grace of life’s later seasons.

Each day is a gift

“Granny, let’s skip!” my granddaughter suggested as she lightly cavorted down the sidewalk.

I followed suit. She stopped, watched me in awe, held her hands over her mouth, and giggled. “That’s not how you skip!”

Since then, I practice my skipping, but my seventy-something hips and knees are stiff, and I just don’t seem to be as coordinated as I was in my youth. Picture an aging giraffe trying to lope down the Serengeti or Grandpa from the 60’s television show The Beverly Hillbillies shuffling along. That’s how graceful I am when I skip.

My lack of coordination is one clue I have joined the older generation. If you are like me, you find yourself at this precarious passage much too soon and unprepared for what may be around the bend.

“Never fear! God is greater than our tomorrows.”

Never fear! God is greater than our tomorrows. We need to face each day with gratitude and with the awareness that, although our longevity may not be what we would like, we have stored a wealth of information through our experiences.

May the pages of my memoir lighten your load, and perhaps we can share some joy, realizing each day is a gift from God.
