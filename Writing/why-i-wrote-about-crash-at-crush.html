Behind the Story

Why I Wrote About the Crash at Crush, Texas

By Karen Hodges

★

A roadside stop for kolaches opened the door to one of Texas history’s strangest true stories.

A bakery wall, a yellowed headline, and an irresistible story

Have you ever heard of anyone planning a train wreck? Well, I have!

One day, my husband and I were on our way to visit our son in North Texas, and we decided to take a break in West, Texas, a Czech community well known for its delicious kolaches. After I had chosen a nice assortment of pastries, I noticed framed copies of yellowed newspapers on the wall of the shop. I studied the pictures while my husband paid the bill.

I asked Mr. Montgomery, the owner of the bakery, about the newspaper articles. He told us about an unbelievable train wreck which took place ten miles north of Waco on September 15, 1896. The headlines on these yellowed newspapers read, “Train Wreck Terror,” “Wreck and Ruin on the Katy,” and “A Pre-arranged Head-on Collision.” Yes, that last headline really caught my eye. I was intrigued by his story, and Mr. Montgomery gave me copies of the newspaper articles of this unbelievable, but true-to-life tale.

“Yes, that last headline really caught my eye.”
