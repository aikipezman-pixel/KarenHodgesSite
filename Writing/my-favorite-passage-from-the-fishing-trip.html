My Favorite Passage from The Fishing Trip

By Karen Hodges

★

A brief reflection on kindness, faith, and the difference one small act can make.

One act of kindness at a time

None of us may be able to solve many of the world’s problems—and they are overwhelming.

But one day at a time, one act of kindness at a time, we can help others. Jesus said to love our neighbors whether that neighbor is a child in another country needing a meal, a senior citizen requiring a ride to the doctor, or perhaps a smelly turtle just wanting to live.

“We can do something for others in need.”

We can do something for others in need.
