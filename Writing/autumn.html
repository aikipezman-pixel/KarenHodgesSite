From Karen’s Notebook

Autumn

By Karen Hodges

★

A quiet meditation on change, aging, beauty, and the desire to let one season linger before the next begins.

Let autumn last a long, long time

We went walking tonight.

The autumn wind
gusted against my cheeks,
cooling, refreshing, and
reminding me of the changing
of the seasons.

I, too, am changing,
passing from one season
of my life to the next.

No longer a life-bearer,
a life-giver,
my body is drying up,
its fruition ended;
but, hopefully,
not all its beauty
will be soon lost,
as trees turn
their brightest colors
in the fall.

The sinister breeze
reminds me of the
coming winter—
hold back,
let autumn last a long, long time,
but not forever.
